IronNotes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

IronNotes is a workout diary. The developer runs no servers that the app talks to, and the app has no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers.

  • What is stored. Your workout log (exercises, sets, weight, reps, notes), body measurements (weight, waist, chest, arm), profile (name, sex, age, height, weight, program settings), custom exercises and programs and, optionally, a profile photo. Everything stays on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync (Pro), the same data is also kept in your private iCloud database (CloudKit) — only you can access it; the developer cannot see it. A short summary for widgets (next workout, this week) is stored in the app's shared folder on the device.
  • What is not collected. The developer receives none of this data, no device identifiers and no usage information.
  • Apple Health (optional, Pro). Only with your permission: the app saves finished workouts (type, duration, estimated calories) to Health and reads your body weight to complete your measurements. Health data never leaves the device, is not shared with the developer or third parties and is not used for advertising. You can revoke access in the Health app.
  • Network. The app does not send your data to the internet. Exceptions: links to technique videos and video search (Rutube, VK Video, YouTube) open in your browser on third-party sites under their own rules when you tap them, and we pass nothing to them; purchases (below) are handled by Apple.
  • Purchases. IronNotes Pro (a one-time purchase) goes through the App Store and your Apple ID. Only Apple receives payment details. The app asks Apple only whether a purchase exists and keeps that on the device; the developer receives neither your Apple ID nor payment details, nor a record of your purchase tied to you. The terms of use are the standard Apple EULA.
  • Profile photo. Chosen with the system photo picker, without access to your whole library; a copy is kept inside the app.
  • Notifications. Permission is used only for the rest-timer sound while the app is in the background. They are local notifications and optional.
  • Files. Excel (.xlsx) and custom-exercise import/export use the system file picker and share sheet: you decide where a file goes.
  • Backups. App data may be included in your device backup (iCloud or computer) according to your iOS settings; that is done by the system, not by the developer.
  • Deletion. Deleting the app erases its data. You can also delete workouts inside the app.
  • Children. The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data about them.
  • Not medical advice. Programs and calculations (for example body mass index) are informational and do not replace a doctor or a coach.
  • Changes. A new version of this policy is published at the same address with a new date.
