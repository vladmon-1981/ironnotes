FAQ

How do I restore IronNotes Pro on a new iPhone?Settings → IronNotes Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.
How do I move my workouts to another device?With Pro, turn on iCloud sync (Settings → Apple Health and iCloud) on both devices with the same Apple ID. Without sync, use Settings → iCloud → backup to a file, then restore from that file on the new device.
What happens to my log after the 14 free days?Every workout you logged stays and remains viewable. Only starting new workouts requires Pro.
Can I import my Excel log?Yes: Settings → Excel import (.xlsx). Sheets and headers in English or Russian are recognized.
How do I delete my data?Your data lives only on your device and in your private iCloud. Deleting the app erases it from the device; synced data can be removed in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.
